# Ask Hajj Kassim

Build a Modern Islamic Q&A Web App — “Ask Hajj Kassim”

Design and build a professional, modern, responsive Islamic question-and-answer web application called “Ask Hajj Kassim.”

Brand

App Name: Ask Hajj Kassim

Tagline:
“Ask. Learn. Understand Islam.”

Alternative tagline:

“Your Digital Companion for Authentic Islamic Knowledge.”

The platform should allow Muslims and anyone interested in Islam to ask questions about any area of Islam, including Qur’an, Hadith, Salah, fasting, Zakat, Hajj, Umrah, marriage, divorce, inheritance, property sharing, business, Islamic manners, family matters, Islamic history, duas, halal and haram, and everyday Islamic questions.

The application should feel trustworthy, peaceful, educational, modern, respectful, and easy to use.

1. Main Concept

Ask Hajj Kassim is an Islamic knowledge platform where users can ask questions in natural language and receive clear, understandable answers based on reliable Islamic sources.

Examples:

“How many rak'ahs are in Fajr prayer?”

“What does the Qur'an say about patience?”

“How should inheritance be divided in Islam?”

“What are the rights of a husband and wife?”

“Can I combine prayers while travelling?”

“What is the ruling on interest in Islam?”

“What does Islam say about business partnerships?”

“How is property shared after someone dies?”

“What is the difference between Zakat and Sadaqah?”

“Can I pray after missing a prayer?”

The system should answer questions while clearly distinguishing between:

Qur'an

Authentic Hadith

Scholarly opinions

Differences between schools of thought

General Islamic guidance

Questions requiring a qualified scholar

2. Important Religious Accuracy Requirement

This is a religious information platform, so accuracy and source transparency are extremely important.

Do NOT present AI-generated information as unquestionable religious authority.

For every answer, provide a Sources & Evidence section where appropriate.

Example:

Answer

Islam encourages Muslims to maintain good relationships with their parents...

Evidence

Qur'an — Surah Al-Isra 17:23

[Reference]

Additional Sources

Qur'an

Sahih al-Bukhari

Sahih Muslim

Other recognized scholarly sources

Add a notice:

Important: Ask Hajj Kassim provides educational Islamic information and is not a replacement for a qualified Islamic scholar or mufti. Matters involving personal fatwas, marriage, divorce, inheritance, business disputes, and other serious legal/religious matters should be verified with a qualified scholar.
